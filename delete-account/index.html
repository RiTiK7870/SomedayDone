SomedayDone

You can delete your SomedayDone account from inside the app. If you no longer have access to the app, you can request deletion by email using the instructions below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to ritikraj5457@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line SomedayDone Account Deletion Request.
  3. Send the request from the Google account email address associated with your SomedayDone account, so we can verify that you own the account. Do not send your password or Google verification codes.
  4. We may reply if additional information is needed to verify the request.

What will be deleted

After we verify your request, we will delete your SomedayDone account and associated app data, including goals, steps and progress stored for that account, subject to any limited retention required by law or reasonably necessary for security or fraud prevention.

Processing

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days. If any information must be retained, we will limit it to the purpose and period required and explain this where applicable.
