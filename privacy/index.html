Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SomedayDone (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the SomedayDone mobile application.

Information we handle

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive account information made available through Google sign-in, such as your name, email address and profile image, as applicable.
  • Content you add: Goals, steps, progress, reminders and other information you choose to enter into SomedayDone.
  • App and device information: Technical information needed for app operation, authentication, security and notifications may be processed by the app or its service providers.

How information is used

We use information to create and maintain your account, sync your goals and progress, provide app features and reminders, troubleshoot issues, protect the service and respond to support requests.

Service providers and sharing

SomedayDone uses Supabase for backend services, including authentication and data storage. Google provides Google Sign-In. These providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own advertising purposes.

Storage and security

Your account and app content may be stored on Supabase infrastructure. We use service-provider security features and reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and deletion

We retain account information and content while your account is active and as needed to operate the service. You can initiate account deletion from within the app. You can also request deletion without the app by following the instructions on our Delete Account page. We will process verified requests and delete associated account data, subject to limited retention where required by law or for legitimate security and fraud-prevention purposes. Any retained data will be limited to what is necessary.

Children

SomedayDone is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices and contact

You may stop using the app and request account deletion. For privacy questions or requests, contact ritikraj5457@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
